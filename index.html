Three questions before you trust an “inflow”

  1. Did system-wide stable float expand, contract, or merely reshuffle?
  2. Did spot conversion into BTC/ETH confirm — or was the move mostly short cover / perp?
  3. Did institutional sleeves agree (e.g. BTC vs ETH ETF flows)?

Until those three are answered, “inflow” is a headline, not a map.
